# SANKET × CivicLens — Data Source Registry

This registry provides the authoritative catalog of all data sources integrated, ingested, or supported by the SANKET × CivicLens platform for the Union Territory of **Chandigarh, India**.

## 1. Overview & Source Taxonomy

All data records within the platform are categorized by authoritative source types:
- `GOVERNMENT`: Official datasets from Union Government of India or State/UT Administrations.
- `MUNICIPAL`: Datasets published by Municipal Corporation Chandigarh (MCC) or Smart City Chandigarh (CCSL).
- `CITIZEN`: Real citizen reports submitted via the CivicLens PWA.
- `FIELD_TEAM`: On-site observations and closure verifications submitted by authorized field personnel.
- `AI_DERIVED`: Predictions generated by SANKET computer vision and NLP models (strictly tagged as suggestions).
- `SYSTEM_DERIVED`: Deterministic metrics calculated from actual verified database records (e.g., Civic Confidence, Risk, Recurrence).

## 3. Data Integrity & Availability Policy
1. When an API endpoint is offline or credentials are not provisioned, SANKET marks the source as `API UNAVAILABLE` or `HISTORICAL DATASET`.
2. Missing historical data or unrecorded municipal zones are displayed as `Historical data unavailable` or `Insufficient historical evidence`. Under no circumstance are mock records fabricated.
